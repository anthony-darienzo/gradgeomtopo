The L-space conjecture, proposed by Boyer, Gordon, Watson, and Juhász, is one of
the central unifying conjectures in low-dimensional topology. It posits that for
an irreducible rational homology 3-sphere, the following three seemingly
disparate conditions are equivalent: (1) it is not an L-space (i.e., its reduced
Heegaard Floer homology is non-trivial); (2) it admits a co-orientable taut
foliation; and (3) its fundamental group is left-orderable.

In this talk, I will provide a technical overview of this conjecture and some
recent progress.
