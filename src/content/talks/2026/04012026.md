Dg-manifolds are used to model homotopy fiber products and infinitesimal
quotients. Therefore, it seems natural to try and build some symplectic geometry
on them. Interestingly, in low degrees one uncovers that symplectic dg-manifolds
correspond to familiar objects, such as Poisson and Courant algebroids. I will
discuss this correspondence and possible applications of differential graded
symplectic geometry.
