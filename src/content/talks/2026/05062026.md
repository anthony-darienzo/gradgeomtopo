In low-dimensions, the dynamics on a manifold strongly dictates the possible
topology. In this talk I will provide an introduction to Anosov flows, which are
the most well-studied example of this phenomenon. We will also discuss the
extension to pseudo-Anosov flows, and mention recent results on these flows.
