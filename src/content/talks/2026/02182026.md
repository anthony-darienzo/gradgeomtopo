On the class “Mfld", smooth manifolds, take one and give it a Riemannian metric.
This yields a metric space. Deforming the Riemannian metric changes this metric
space; deforming it enough changes the topology. Cheeger and Colding’s 1997
paper shows us what lies at the limit of these deformations. They are not
manifolds, but they are close. In this talk I’ll summarize Cheeger and Colding’s
results. Afterwards, we will see an example of the kind of limiting space at the
boundary of “Mfld”. This is a type of singular space, and I will show how this
singular space evades canonical generalizations of the class of manifolds. This
talk will be suited for a general audience.
