In optimal control, one is tasked with finding trajectories of a dynamical
system (with inputs) that extremize a certain cost, or action, functional. The
maximum principle (a la Pontryagin) translates this into a Hamiltonian system on
a (pre-)symplectic manifold. In the presence of symmetries, we can use this
perspective to obtain conservation laws (a la Noether, or more aptly, Sussmann)
satisfied along extremal trajectories, however, if we would like to understand
optimal trajectories on the “reduced” space, we are naturally lead to studying
control problems on Lie algebroids (as initiated by Weinstein for Lagrangian
mechanics and Martinez/Cortes for control).
