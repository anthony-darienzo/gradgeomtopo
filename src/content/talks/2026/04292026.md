In this talk, I will discuss a family of what are supposed to be complete
extremal Kahler surfaces. This family at rational values defines an orbifold
solutions, and at irrational values quasifold solutions. However, there seems to
be a smooth family of Riemannian foliations whose leaf spaces are exactly the
orbifold or quasifold surfaces of the family. On the level of surfaces, all
members of the family have a representation by a metric on a sphere with conical
singularities, whose cone angle is rational when the member is an orbifold, and
irrational when the member is a quasifold.

Since the Riemannian foliation seems to be the fundamental object here, I will
discuss some aspects of Molino’s structure theory for Riemannian foliations.
This has some consequences for the type of conical metrics the Riemannian
foliation can represent. For example, I think Molino theory implies some
symmetry of the singular metric on the quasifold. I will also explain how
Riemannian foliations give rise to metrics on orbifolds and quasifolds.
Everything will be motivated from the example which I hope keeps it accessible.
