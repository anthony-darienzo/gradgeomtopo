A cone is a type of singularity on a manifold, suggesting that the smooth
structure should be replaced with something else, adapted to the singularity.
For example, near isotropy points of a G-orbifold, the structure sheaf is
comprised by the G-equivariant functions.

There is a menagerie of candidate models. I will discuss a few of these models
and how each describes a conical singularity in their own way. the key example
comes from an arXiv [note](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.03993) describing
a spectral triple for a conical singularity. This is based on older work by
Connes and Moscovici.
