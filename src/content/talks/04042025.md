In my talk, I will explain that ghosts (and anti-ghosts) are not scary; rather,
they are tools created by physicists to work with symplectic reduction. The idea
is to resolve the reduction using the BRST complex, which naturally carries a
Poisson structure. I will also explore why this approach breaks down when the
reduction is singular and discuss potential ways to fix it.
