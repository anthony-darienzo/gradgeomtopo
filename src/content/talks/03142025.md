One often studies surfaces by cutting along nice curves; the original surface is
then recovered by appropriately gluing the resulting disks. It's likewise common
in 3-manifold theory to cut along nice surfaces and study the geometry of the
pieces. One important question in dimensions 2 and 3 is knowing how many times
we must cut until all topology trivializes (i.e., until we get only disks or
balls). In this talk, I will discuss both the history of this problem, as well
as recent results in dimension 3, including work of the speaker.
