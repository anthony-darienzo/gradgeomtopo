The Jones polynomial is a well-known link invariant, discovered by
Vaughan Jones in the 80s. It is very handy in low-dimensional
topology, as it admits an easy combinatorial method for computation.
We begin by describing how to compute it via the Kaufmann state model
and cube of resolutions. We then proceed to construct a homology
theory, known as Khovanov homology, whose graded Euler characteristic
is the Jones polynomial and which gives a stronger invariant. This
provides a glimpse into the vast world of topological quantum field
theories (TQFTs) and categorification.
