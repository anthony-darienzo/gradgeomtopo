In this talk, we will discuss the boundary asymptotic behavior of
conformally compact Einstein metrics, with a focus on the
Fefferman-Graham expansion. This formal series expansion reveals that
all odd terms up to order n−1 vanish, leaving even terms as dominant
in the asymptotic behavior. The coefficients of the expansion are
locally determined by boundary data up to order n−1, while the n-th
order term, which is not locally determined, plays the role of the
Neumann data. This naturally leads to the study of the
Dirichlet-to-Neumann map, whose linearization is closely related to
powers of a Laplace-type operator
