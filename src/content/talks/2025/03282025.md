Quantum knot invariants are known to come from R-matrices along with some extra
structures, a process called the Reshetikhin-Turaev functor. In 2019, Rinat
Kashaev proved that R-matrices alone are sufficient to define knot invariants,
as long as they satisfy some nondegeneracy conditions called rigidity. More
recently, Stavros Garoufalidis and Rinat Kashaev developed a new method of
constructing rigid R-matrices, which recovers several known knot polynomials
such as the colored Jones polynomials, and gives a new family of multivariable
knot polynomials, the V_n-polynomials. In this talk, I will talk about the
Reshetikhin-Turaev functor in this context, the computation of V_n polynomials
and the patterns of the results based on 361404 knots computed. Joint work with
Stavros Garoufalidis.
