It's been a while that a symplectic manifold has come up in this seminar. In
this talk I actually want to go a bit further and look at Poisson manifolds, and
what Lagrangians in that setting look like. Some things might look familiar from
symplectic geometry, but Lagrangians in Poisson manifolds, just like their
ambient spaces, are rather wild objects. Nevertheless, if we use Lie algebroids
smartly, we can still under some conditions prove versions of Weinstein's
Lagrangian neighborhood theorem, using techniques such as Moser arguments, which
is cool!
